## Design Context

### Users
Executive stakeholders reviewing the economic value of Eni, Plenitude and Enilive, with enough analytical detail to understand the evidence. The user confirmed: “mostly executive but a bit more analytics is good”.

### Brand Personality
Authoritative, clear, analytical. Retain the existing OpenEconomics presentation identity and Italian copy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve existing fonts, navy/yellow palette, brand colors, logos, chapter structure and presentation styling. Improvements concern clarity, provenance, legibility and company identification.

### Design Principles
- Explain financial terms before using them.
- Lead with the finding, then explain how to read the figure.
- Identify the company and geography in every relevant subsection.
- Distinguish source figures, calculated summaries and source inconsistencies.
- Preserve branding; do not redesign the presentation.
